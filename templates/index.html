How the algorithms work

First-Fit

Gives each arriving vertex the smallest color not already used by one of its earlier neighbours. It is simple and usually fine, but an adversary can make it use n/2 colors on a graph that needs 2.

CBIP (Coloring Based on Interval Partitioning)

Works on bipartite graphs. When a vertex arrives it splits its connected component into two sides and gives the vertex the smallest color not used on the opposite side. That keeps it to O(log n) colors.
